## Purpose

This set keeps pure geometry primitives, notation, relations, constructions, and decomposition crates separate from the broader `use-math` surface while preserving a thin geometry facade for one-dependency use cases.

## Why this shape

Keeping geometry in its own set keeps the facade thin, lets focused child crates grow without turning the facade into a monolith, and keeps geometry naming separate from the broader math facade.
