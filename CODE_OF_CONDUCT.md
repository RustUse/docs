# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in the RustUse docs project should be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Give technical feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Assume good intent while still holding a high bar for quality.
- Be respectful of different experience levels, communication styles, and backgrounds.
- Focus discussion on the work rather than the person.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, dogpiling, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.

## Scope

This applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or moderate content that violates this code of conduct, and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

If you experience or witness a serious problem, report it privately to the maintainer instead of escalating in public.
